Light Segment

光段

Year
2017
Type
Light Installation
Materials
7 segment light, light stand, angle steel, self-designed PCB board — 175 × 35 × 30 cm
With
LLL (林瑜亮 Lin Yu-Liang, 李翎暄 Li Ling-Syuan)

Imagining dynamic information's movement and flows in the Digital Era. One after another, the numerical increase and decrease. As the events happened in modern time, one after another. Expectable, but at the same time difficult to search for the starting point. We try to mimic this conception with massive numbers counting down.

In the work, people can see every number as a “pixel” representing the remaining time of the light point — countdown from 9 until the extinguishing of itself. Between the micro and the overview point, glowing the illuminations of lights and shadows. We expect to present a dazzling light performance in a distance. At the same time, every operation can be seen in detail by a closer look.

我們想像數位時代的流動資訊不斷變動與轉化，一盞接著一盞數值的消長，如同數位時代的事件不斷地發生，一件接著一件，有所預期，卻又無法尋覓其開端，我們以大量的倒數數字呈現這樣流動狀態的意象。

作品中每一格「像素」中的數字，皆代表畫面光點殘留的時間；自 9 開始倒數至光點熄滅的時間。而微觀與綜觀之間的光影變幻，我們希望營造出遠觀下光影虛幻之間的炫目變化。同時在微觀注目下，可以看見每一個細部的運作。
