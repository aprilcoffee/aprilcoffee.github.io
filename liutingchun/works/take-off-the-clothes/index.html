Take Off the Clothes

脫衣服

Year
2017
Type
Performance Record

Instruction: Take off the clothes, seek from the inner sound.

The piece was performed by the time I was exchanging in AVU, Prague. During the exchange, I was unfamiliar with all the things happening around me, cultures, languages, even bodies. Then I started working with actions. It was a shock to know how little I knew about the feelings of my own body.

The weather was cold in Europe, I wore a lot more than I usually did in Taiwan. This started the idea of this performance, I tried to undress in public, seeking the difference between who I was, who I am, and the audiences on-site.

Later on, when I was back to Taiwan, I did the same performance again, trying to figure it out in a different context.

Performer: Ting-Chun Liu
