# LLL (Lin Yu-Liang, Li Ling-Syuan)

LLL is the collective of Ting-Chun Liu with 林瑜亮 Lin Yu-Liang and 李翎暄 Li Ling-Syuan, making light
installations with seven-segment displays.

- [Light Segment](../works/light-segment.md) (2017)
- [Segmentary Moonlight](../works/segmentary-moonlight.md) (2018, Yue-Jin Lantern Festival)
