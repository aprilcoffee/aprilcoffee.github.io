# Leon-Etienne Kühr

Artist and long-time collaborator of Ting-Chun Liu. Together they make installations, curatorial
projects and many talks about generative AI.

## Joint works

- [Steering Through the Inner Residue](../works/steering-through-the-inner-residue.md) (2026)
- [Interpolation of the Invisible Color](../works/interpolation-of-the-invisible-color.md) (2025)
- [Self-cannibalizing AI](../works/self-cannibalizing-ai.md) (2023, talk at 37C3)

## Joint talks and exhibitions (from the CV)

- Chaos Communication Congress: 37C3 (2023), 38C3 (2024), 39C3 (2025)
- TEDA'24 at the University of Cambridge (2024); Screen Walks, London (2025)
- ACM SIGGRAPH Digital Arts Community, KISD Cologne, Storage Museum and Filmwerkstatt Düsseldorf, mtrail camp Lindau (2026)
- *AI-Worlding* exhibitions in Darmstadt and Frankfurt (2026)
