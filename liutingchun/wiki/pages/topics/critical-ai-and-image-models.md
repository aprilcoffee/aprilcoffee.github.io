# Critical AI and image models

Since about 2022 the centre of Liu's practice is generative AI, approached as an artist and programmer
who opens up the "black box". Strategies named in the work texts are feedback, misuse and hacking:
feeding models their own output, taking text-to-image pipelines apart, labelling and scoring images,
and probing models such as CLIP for stereotypes.

## Works

- [Self-cannibalizing AI](../works/self-cannibalizing-ai.md): talk at 37C3 on exposing Stable Diffusion pipelines, aesthetic scoring, NSFW filters and CLIP bias.
- [Imaginary Landscape](../works/imaginary-landscape.md): idealised beach images, social-media bias and image generation.
- [Just Jailbreak](../works/just-jailbreak.md): a game about "jailbreaking" a language model.
- [Interpolation of the Invisible Color](../works/interpolation-of-the-invisible-color.md): embeddings used to curate a museum collection, and their limits.
- [Steering Through the Inner Residue](../works/steering-through-the-inner-residue.md): an image model steered only by its own output.
