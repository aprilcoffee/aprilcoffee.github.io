# The material infrastructure of AI

AI looks immaterial but runs on graphics cards, electricity and semiconductor supply chains, many of
which lead back to Taiwan. Liu's work makes this visible through heat: computation's waste heat as
image material, and the phone warming in your hand. The perspective is explicitly that of a Taiwanese
artist, limited in computing power yet connected to the infrastructure global AI depends on.

## Works and research

- [Latent Heat Generation](../works/latent-heat-generation.md): thermal feedback between GPUs and Stable Diffusion; research notes at https://aprilcoffee.github.io/heat_as_image/
- [Sun](../works/sun.md) (2020): an early piece in which a web page heats the phone.
