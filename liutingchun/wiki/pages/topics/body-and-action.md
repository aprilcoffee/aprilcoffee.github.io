# Body and action

Around 2016–2020, during studies at TNUA and an exchange at AVU in Prague, much of the work started
from the body: repeating a gesture until it misaligns, undressing in public, following machine
instructions. The 2018 blog post "身體作為現場的折射（一）" (Body as the reflection on site) traces this period.
