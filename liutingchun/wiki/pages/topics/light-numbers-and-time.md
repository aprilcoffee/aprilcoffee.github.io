# Light, numbers and time

Light and sound installations, several made with the collective LLL, that turn digits and countdowns
into images of time. The blog post on shift registers and seven-segment displays (2019) documents the
electronics behind them.

- [Light Segment](../works/light-segment.md) (2017): seven-segment "pixels" counting down from 9.
- [Segmentary Moonlight](../works/segmentary-moonlight.md) (2018): a full moon of seven-segment displays for the Yue-Jin Lantern Festival, Tainan.
- [Beneath](../works/beneath.md) (2022): directional sound of waves through a skylight.
