# Internet and network art

Works that use the web itself as material: the hyperlink, the social-media post, the phone in the hand.

- [Hyper-Link into Image](../works/hyper-link-into-image.md) (2016): a router turns every web page into a static image.
- [Message in a Bottle](../works/message-in-a-bottle.md) (2017): Facebook posts that contain only their own timestamp.
- [Sun](../works/sun.md) (2020): a sun in the mobile browser that heats the phone.
