# Archives and institutions

Two works look at how museums order, keep and give meaning to objects, and at the people whose labour
holds a collection together.

- [Interpolation of the Invisible Color](../works/interpolation-of-the-invisible-color.md): the Storage Museum's 479 works mapped by embeddings; the staff's shelf order is treated as
  embodied knowledge, and the exhibition text insists that "only you can decide what matters".
- [Achtung! Verwischung](../works/achtung-verwischung.md): wet-floor stands and mopping at the Rautenstrauch-Joest-Museum, Cologne, during the
  "Leaky Archive" seminar; about restitution, roles and the one-sidedness of institutions.

Related talks: *Generating for the Archive* and *Latenz*, both at the Storage Museum Düsseldorf (2026).
