# Recursion and feedback

A recurring method: let a system confront its own output and watch where it drifts. The work texts
describe recursion as "a spiral that surpasses linear causation", and feedback as the microphone held
to a speaker, where the result shows the room as much as the machine. Alvin Lucier's *I Am Sitting in
a Room* (1969) is a named reference.

## Works

- [Steering Through the Inner Residue](../works/steering-through-the-inner-residue.md): image generation without prompts, guided only by previous images.
- [Latent Heat Generation](../works/latent-heat-generation.md): GPUs reading their own heat and turning it into images.
- [Self-cannibalizing AI](../works/self-cannibalizing-ai.md): models learning from each other's output.
