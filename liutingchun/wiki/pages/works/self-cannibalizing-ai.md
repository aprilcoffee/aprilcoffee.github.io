# Self-cannibalizing AI

A talk with Leon-Etienne Kühr at the 37th Chaos Communication Congress (37C3, 2023) on artistic strategies (feedback, misuse, hacking) for opening up text-to-image models. It covers feeding models their own output, deconstructing Stable Diffusion pipelines, aesthetic scoring in public datasets, NSFW classification and CLIP biases.

## Key ideas

- Image models look like black boxes but are chains of models, datasets and filters.
- When models learn from each other's output, they 'cannibalize' themselves.
- CLIP correlations expose problematic stereotypes in everyday tools.
