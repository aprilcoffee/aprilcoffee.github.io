# Imaginary Landscape

An ongoing project (2022–23) about the idealised beach images of tourism and social media, developed with image-generation models and presented as audio-visual performances. Collectively imagined 'perfect beaches' are pieced together by AI into an unreachable 'ideal other side'.

## Key ideas

- The beach became desirable only with modernity (Alain Corbin, *The Lure of the Sea*); today it is terraformed to match photos.
- Training data from social media carries representation and evaluation bias, so models produce banal beach clichés.
- Grew out of the Xanadu Travel Agency (仙納度旅行社) postcard project, described in a 2022 blog post.
