# I kept repeating the unfamiliar self (我不斷重複不認識的自己)

A video installation made during an exchange in Prague (2017). One simple gesture that produces a sound was performed more than a hundred times; a few frames from each take are compressed into a single movement, exposing the body's misalignment and questioning the authenticity of moving images.

## Key ideas

- Without language and culture, only the body remains.
- Moving images as stacked frames of image and sound.
