# Inevitable Cross of The Parallel (無法避免—平行線的交錯)

A performance (2017) with Yu-Wei He. Two computers play a game whose moves are voiced as instructions; the two performers must follow them on a taped floor, and in a second act retrace the recorded path. Organic movement and artificial voice compete and correspond.

## Key ideas

- Rules in digital relations: obey or confront them.
- Infinite personal possibilities inside fixed rules.
