# Beneath

A sound installation with directional speakers and LED strips. The sound of ocean waves reflected off a skylight makes the submarine-like building feel underwater, connecting inside and outside.

## Facts

- **Year:** 2022
- **Type:** Sound Installation
- **Type (de):** Klanginstallation
- **Type (zh):** 聲音裝置
- **Materials:** Directional Speaker, LED Light strips
- **Page:** [https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/works/beneath/](https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/works/beneath/) · [de](https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/de/works/beneath/) · [zh](https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/zh/works/beneath/)

## Key ideas

- Skylights carry the wish to look upward; here they carry sound downward.
