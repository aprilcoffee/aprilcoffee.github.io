# Sun (太陽)

A piece of internet art: a p5.js web page that shows a sun on a mobile phone while making the phone warm. Opened in a mobile browser, it blurs the line between the digital image and physical sensation. Dedicated to the memory of Ying Wen, Wang.

## Facts

- **Year:** 2020
- **Type:** Internet Art (p5.js website)
- **Type (de):** Netzkunst (p5.js-Website)
- **Type (zh):** 網路藝術（p5.js 網頁）
- **Credits:** In memory of Ying Wen, Wang
- **Link:** [Open the work (mobile browser)](https://aprilcoffee.github.io/sun/)
- **Page:** [https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/works/sun/](https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/works/sun/) · [de](https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/de/works/sun/) · [zh](https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/zh/works/sun/)

## Key ideas

- Which sense do we use to understand something on the internet? Here: touch and heat.
- Heat as the footprint of computation, an idea later developed in Latent Heat Generation.
