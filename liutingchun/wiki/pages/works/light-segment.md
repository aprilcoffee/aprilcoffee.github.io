# Light Segment (光段)

A light installation by LLL (2017) made of seven-segment displays on a custom PCB. Every digit is a 'pixel' counting down from 9 until its light goes out: a dazzling field from afar, readable mechanics up close, an image of information flowing in the digital era.

## Facts

- **Year:** 2017
- **Type:** Light Installation
- **Type (de):** Lichtinstallation
- **Type (zh):** 燈光裝置
- **Materials:** 7 segment light, light stand, angle steel, self-designed PCB board — 175 × 35 × 30 cm
- **With:** LLL (林瑜亮 Lin Yu-Liang, 李翎暄 Li Ling-Syuan)
- **Page:** [https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/works/light-segment/](https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/works/light-segment/) · [de](https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/de/works/light-segment/) · [zh](https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/zh/works/light-segment/)

## Key ideas

- Countdown as an image of events following one another without a visible beginning.
