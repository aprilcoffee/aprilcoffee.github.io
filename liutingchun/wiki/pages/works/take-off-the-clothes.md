# Take Off the Clothes (脫衣服)

A performance first made at AVU in Prague and later repeated in Taiwan. The instruction: take off the clothes, seek from the inner sound. It started from wearing far more clothes in the European winter and asks how the performer's past and present self relate to the audience.
