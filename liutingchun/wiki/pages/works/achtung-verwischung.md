# Achtung! Verwischung

A site-specific performance made during the 'Leaky Archive' seminar (2023) for an exhibition at the Rautenstrauch-Joest-Museum (RJM) in Cologne. Custom wet-floor stands and the act of mopping an already clean floor turned the museum's leaks, and its one-sided logic of 'you can contribute but not withdraw', into the work.

## Key ideas

- 'Verwischung' means blurring or obliteration; without the prefix, 'Wischung' suggests mopping.
- Questions about ethnographic museums, restitution (Benin bronzes) and the role of student artists.
- Conversations with ticket and cleaning staff shaped the piece.
