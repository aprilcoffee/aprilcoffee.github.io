# Segmentary Moonlight (月光・段)

A light installation by the collective LLL for the Yue-Jin Lantern Festival in Yanshui, Tainan (2018): seven-segment displays form a full moon, answering the crescent shape of the district, and join the ancient time-keeping moon with digital numbers.

## Facts

- **Year:** 2018
- **Type:** Light Installation
- **Type (de):** Lichtinstallation
- **Type (zh):** 燈光裝置
- **Materials:** 7 segment, scaffolding, wood board — 200 × 100 × 250 cm
- **With:** LLL (林瑜亮 Lin Yu-Liang, 李翎暄 Li Ling-Syuan)
- **Page:** [https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/works/segmentary-moonlight/](https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/works/segmentary-moonlight/) · [de](https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/de/works/segmentary-moonlight/) · [zh](https://aprilcoffee.github.io/liutingchun/zh/works/segmentary-moonlight/)

## Key ideas

- The moon as an old clock; numbers as today's time.
