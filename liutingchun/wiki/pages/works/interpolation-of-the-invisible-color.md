# Interpolation of the Invisible Color

A curatorial project and interactive installation with Leon-Etienne Kühr, commissioned by the Storage Museum Düsseldorf. The museum's 479 works, kept on 10 shelves, were mapped with embeddings that ran entirely locally, so visitors could wander the collection by machine-measured similarity, while the project insists that correlation is not meaning.

## Key ideas

- Duchamp's 'invisible color': titles, labels and histories colour how an artwork is seen.
- The shelf order is an archive of the staff's embodied knowledge; the project credits the staff by name.
- "AI is simple": it finds correlations, not causes; people project meaning into the constellation.
- A note for participating artists and visitors (EN/DE) was part of the exhibition.
