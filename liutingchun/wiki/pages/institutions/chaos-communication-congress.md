# Chaos Communication Congress

The annual congress of the Chaos Computer Club. Liu and Leon-Etienne Kühr spoke there three years in a row:
- 37C3 (2023): *Self-cannibalizing AI – Artistic Strategies to Expose Generative Text-to-Image Models* → [Self-cannibalizing AI](../works/self-cannibalizing-ai.md)
- 38C3 (2024): *arafed futures – An Artist Dialogue on Chip Storage and AI Accelerationism*
- 39C3 (2025): *51 Ways to Spell the Image Giraffe: The Hidden Politics of Token Languages in Generative AI*
